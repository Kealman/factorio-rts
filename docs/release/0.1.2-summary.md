# Release 0.1.2: summary for the owner

Date 2026-10-26. Base 0.1.1 (43a8581). Factorio 2.1.23. This page is written from the four agents' notes (`docs/qa/release-0-1-2-r3-*-notes.md`); the lead's own acceptance numbers on the merged tree (release check, identity, owner save) are not in it yet: fill in the "Merged tree" block below.

## What changed, per issue

| Issue | In plain words |
|---|---|
| #22 craft queue | Factory and barracks buttons order crafts: click 1, Shift 5, Ctrl as many as the network can pay, right click takes some off. A finished order stops the machine. An **Endless** toggle keeps the old behaviour. |
| #23 demolish pieces | Belts, underground belts, splitters, inserters and medium poles can be selected (click, or a box with no units in it) and demolished with two presses of Delete. Half the recipe comes back, rounded down per batch: 10 belts give 5 iron, one belt gives nothing. |
| #27 cancel ghosts | A selected ghost is cancelled at once with Delete, Backspace or Cancel. |
| #25 new items | The factory crafts underground belts and splitters. The long-handed inserter is **Nation only**: the Skirmish factory page has no room for a third row. |
| #24 clear order | Right click a tree or rock with units selected, or Shift + right-drag over an area. Rocks pay stone and coal. A ghost placed over trees marks them for the robots. Four walkers cleared 20 trees in 6.5 s. |
| #21 supply hauler coal | Your own supply haulers wait for a fuller load of rations, goods and magazines while the town holds at least half its Keep, 4 minutes at most. The AI is unchanged (the decision of the lead: the hold applies only to forces without an AI commander). In the first, wider version the manual routes' coal fell 10 to 30 % but train items moved with the noise, which is why the hold is limited to your own routes. |
| #26 new city | The settlers bring iron 100, copper 40, stone 40, coal 20, a factory, 2 houses and a farm from the mother city's network, only as far as it holds them (nothing is created). A city whose robots have nothing to build with says "N ghosts waiting: nothing to build them with" and raises an alert. The optional robot recipe was not done. |
| #28 oil | Pumpjack, refinery and chemical plant panels, tooltips, F11 and the R message say that oil travels as items. A pumpjack outside every network shows "no network" and raises an alert. The vanilla art keeps its drawn pipe stub (needs new art). |
| #29 recipe names | Ten recipes had no name key ("Unknown key: recipe-name.rts-oil-processing" and nine more). All have EN and RU names; the locale probe now finds 0 untranslated recipes (was 10 plus two prefix keys). |
| #30 help cards | Hold Shift over anything (LSHIFT or RSHIFT), click **?** on a building, hub, depot or city panel, or press Shift+F11. 85 entries, numbers generated from the game's balance, EN and RU. Build tooltips show a description. A hover card closes with a click, after 20 s or on the next Shift press (the engine gives no key release). |
| #19 objectives log | The other mode's objective steps no longer log "step done". |
| #31 part B stop lists | Trains run stop lists on the engine's native schedule: right click depots to add stops (Shift inserts), per-stop unload, load, troops and bombard orders and the vanilla wait conditions. A 0.1.1 route becomes a two-stop list when the save loads. |
| #32 train builder | Compose a train carriage by carriage with presets and up to 5 templates; platforms of 40, 60 or 80 tiles hold 5, 8 or 11 carriages; couple or drop a carriage at a standing train. |
| #31 part A own track | The vanilla rail planner does not run in the RTS view (checked with real input), so there is a script **Rail tool**: waypoints, curves, branches, a bill and undo. Player track gets chain signals only. Rail stations, rail and chain signals in the build menu, auto lines that reuse your track, any number of lines per depot. |
