Date 2026-10-28. Base 0.1.2 (d51b6ab, mod tree ac55c09). Factorio 2.1.23. This page is written from the three agents' notes (`docs/qa/release-0-1-3-r4-{rails,hud,ai}-notes.md`), the plan (`docs/qa/release-0-1-3-plan.md`) and the release GUI pass (`build/rel-gui-0.1.3-report.md` in the main checkout). ## What changed, per issue

| Issue | In plain words |
|---|---|
| #37 rail planner | Your own track is laid with the **vanilla rail planner** again. The cause of 0.1.2's failure was one setting: the RTS view had hover selection off, and the planner starts from the rail end under the pointer. It is on now while a rail is in your hand. Press the Rail button, click a rail end, then click where the track goes: the path with curves and diagonals follows the pointer. A click on a rail end in the middle makes a branch, track laid onto track joins it, R turns the first rail, Q stops the path (Q again or a right click ends laying). The 2.x planner builds on the click, not while dragging. Robots and construction trains build the ghosts; every junction gets chain signals. Ground track only: bridges stay with the auto lines. The Rail tool, its panel and its Backspace undo are removed. **Your 0.1.2 save:** the Rail tool segments still being built became one planner line (456 ghosts), built pieces stay track. |
| #33 rails follow-ups | The train panel marks a stop that no track reaches from the stop before it (red, "No track leads here from stop N"), and a second train between the same stops brings the passing-loop hint. Player track no longer comes back after its ghosts are cancelled. Track refused on foreign land shows one message per path. |
| #35 factory page | Pause, Auto and Endless are in the page header. A tab that needs more rows makes the panel taller and moves it up, so the status line, counts and queue always show. |
| #36 release numbers | The only player text with a release number was the Endless tooltip ("as in 0.1.1"). It is fixed, and the locale check now fails on release numbers, issue numbers and phase names. |
| #38 top strip | A gear button opens a chooser (any item with stock, power, robots, treasury, supply, army counters, timer; order, remove, reset; at most 12). Defaults per mode: Nation = timer, iron, copper, stone, coal, grain, rations, power, treasury; Skirmish = timer, army counters, supply, iron, copper, stone, coal, magazines, power. Hover shows the total, the split per logistic network and the last minute's change; rations and grain show the minutes left (yellow under 10, red under 3). |
| #34 cosmetics | The train and depot panels stop above the Build menu. Help cards for a selected unit type (? on the unit page) and for the five abilities (? on the ability bar). The long-handed inserter stays Nation only (a 29th Skirmish recipe would grow the panel). The flaky `pump_states` probe check waits longer. |
| #20 AI follow-ups | Siege kits are requested from plan time, from any own city; a lost line's depot rebuild is not held by the engine focus; Industrialisation, then Railways, come before the aggressive AI's army technologies (item 2). Item 4 (army pool passes on the scheduler) is not needed: the perf scene is under its limit. |
| #2 AI rail linking | Partial. Nations linked by 35:00: 12 of 20 (0.1.2: 9; target 16). AI capitals add a coal extractor when coal runs short, a freight train that loses a locomotive or its path is scrapped and rebuilt, the engine focus does not hold the edge that joins a third city. The limit is Age I copper: moved to #39 (0.1.4). |
