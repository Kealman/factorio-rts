## Design

### Extended plans

A plan is *extended* (`P.xl`) when it has 5-6 nations or a 4096 map. Every M3 rule in `world.lua` is gated by it, so a
plan of 2-4 nations up to 3072 is drawn exactly as in M2 (same random draws, same slot budget, same re-rolls, same
climate anchors). The slot budget needs care: with more slots a seed that M2 re-rolled for want of a slot would pass
and give another world, so non-extended plans keep `world.SLOTS_M2` for the budget and the climate and lake caps.

| Rule | 2-4 nations, <= 3072 (M2) | Extended |
|---|---|---|
| Spawn ring | 0.32 / 0.30 / 0.31 x size | 0.32 (5), 0.33 (6) |
| Specialty, home oil | 72 / 78 degrees off outward, 380 / 300 tiles x size/2048 | 50 / 56 degrees (60-72 degree neighbours put a 72 degree specialty on the barrier), distances x min(size/2048, 1.5) |
| Specialty kinds | iron, copper, coal, mixed | the four again for nations 5 and 6 (permutation search over 720 orders) |
| Contested majors | 2-4 bisectors | every bisector, one copper per two nations |
| Centre oil wells | 8 | 9 / 10 |
| Regions | 20 (2048), 28 (3072), 300 tiles apart | 44 (4096) + 4 per nation above 4, 300 x min(size/2048, 1.5) apart |
| Massifs | 2 | 4 on 4096 |
| City-states, uranium, ports | 6, 2, 4 at 4 nations | 7 / 8, 2 / 3, 5 / 6 |
| Ruins, rare, city sites | 12, 8, 48 at 3072 | 16, 10, 64 (+4 per nation above 4) at 4096 |
| Validation | 4-connected flood of the 8-tile grid per spawn | one flood (connectivity) and the octile fields of the 16-tile grid per spawn for scores and nearest-enemy paths, reused by the M2 steps (validate 1.05 -> 0.49 s, M2 context 0.51 -> 0.20 s at 4096 x 6) |

Planning time at 4096 x 6 (load 7-10): skeleton 0.06 s, thickness 0.6 s, sampling 0.55 s, validation 0.49 s, M2 steps
1.0 s; 2.5-3.2 s in all (4 nations 2.2-2.8 s, 5 nations 3.8-4.1 s, when the thickness check needs a third round).

### The plan's chunk index

`worldgen.index(plan)` (inside `worldgen.plan`) builds `plan.features` (world.build_features, now at planning) and gives
each feature `f.chunks` (the in-map chunks of its tile box); `plan.labels` holds one label per region centre, choke and
port with its chunk; `plan.chunk_index[key] = {f = {feature indices}, l = {label indices}}` (key = world.chunk_key).
`plan.core` lists the forced chunks and `plan.site_chunks` the city-state and port squares. Everything is in
`storage.world.plan` (`storage.world.features` is the same table), so a save carries it.

### Generation

1. **Core** (`worldgen.generate_core`): every chunk whose centre lies within 5 chunks of a spawn's chunk (81 chunks per
   spawn, at least 115 tiles around the spawn; the start kit reaches 80, the cleared area 100) is requested and forced.
   Then the spawn areas are cleared and every feature whose chunks exist is placed (the start kits).
2. **Background order** (`worldgen.request_rest`): the corners of the 11 x 11 spawn squares (what the start view and the
   start chart reach), the city-state squares (radius 3), the port squares (radius 1), every other chunk holding a
   feature or a label (in ring order), then the remaining chunks ring by ring: a multi-source, 8-connected BFS over the
   chunk grid from the spawns' chunks. Generated chunks are left out.
3. **Window** (`worldgen.refill`, every 15 ticks): 160 chunks in flight, each a radius-0 request. The engine fills
   42-45 chunks per game second at 60 UPS here (the study measured 39), so the window holds about 4 s of work.
4. **Placement** (`on_chunk_generated`): the chunk is counted once (`storage.worldgen.gen`); every feature of the chunk
   whose chunks all exist is placed through `world.place_feature` (ore with exact totals, oil, ruins, ports as trade
   posts, city-states through `cities.found`); its labels go to `strategic.lua` with the signal `rts_world_labels`;
   the last chunk runs `world.finish_fill` (the real-tile check follows over the next ticks).
5. **Safety nets**: a chunk found generated without an event is counted when the order reaches it; when the order is
   used up and the map is still short, a sweep requests every missing chunk; with no chunk for 60 s the chunks in
   flight are requested again (a save whose engine queue was lost). Neither fired in any run.
6. **Save and load**: the plan, the order, the cursor and the chunks in flight live in `storage.worldgen`; the engine
   keeps its requests across a save (W1 gotcha 10). A save from before this module that is still filling is adopted
   (`worldgen.init`, from `match.init` or the first requester tick): its plan is indexed and the rest requested.

### Legacy order for the M1/M2 worlds

Worlds of 2-4 nations up to 2048 are generated in M2's order, request for request: a 13 x 13 square around every
spawn forced, then the city-states' 3 x 3 chunks, then the whole map in one request (M2's watchdog included). The
reason is measured: the engine's generation order shows at chunk seams.

- **Bench** (`world3_orderbench.lua`, one 2048 plan on fresh surfaces): all at once vs. spawn squares first vs. discs and
  rings give the same ores, mountains and cliffs but differ by 2 marsh and water tiles, up to 6 trees, 1 rock and 46
  decoratives; the same order twice gives the same counts.
- **Seed matrix with the new order at 2048** (20 seeds x 4 nations): plans and sites identical, every world's counts
  slightly different (at most 0.05 % mountain, 0.12 % ford, 0.26 % cliffs, 0.02 % trees; ore entities identical).
- **M1 acceptance with the new order** (seed 501): 52/53. The same cities, towns, lines and Age II times, but in the war
  at minute 26 rts-ai-1 destroyed rts-player's capital depot at minute 36, its train stopped after 22 trips, and the
  train goal failed (18,479 of 20,000 items; the `mods/` runs: 26,612).
- **With the legacy order**: 20 of 20 worlds have the tile and entity counts of the M2 tree, and the M1 acceptance logs
  the same 54 check lines as `qf-m1acc` and `m2i-t16-501`.

Bigger worlds use the new pipeline: at 3072 M2's single whole-map request held back the first background chunk for
20-30 s (W1 gotcha 11); the window starts at once (the city-states of a 3072 world are founded 1.8-9.4 s in).

### Labels by chunk

`strategic.refresh` draws a region name, a choke marker or a port name only when the chunk under it exists
(`worldgen.generated`); the others are drawn for every refreshed force when `rts_world_labels` arrives. The probe
checks the counts per force after the fill without any refresh: 49 regions, 18 chokes and 6 ports for each of the six
forces at 4096.

### HUD and lobby

- **Lobby** (Nation mode): "Nations" 2-6 replaces "Opponents" (item i = i opponents); 5-6 nations move a 2048 choice
  to 3072, picking 2048 caps the nations at 4; "Start map: Explore | Survey"; a dim line under the grid: "World
  4096x4096 (16384 chunks): the land around every capital, city-state and port is ready when the match starts; the
  rest is generated in the background in about 7 min ..." (minutes at 42 chunks per second). The mode and size tooltips name the M3 sizes (new keys; the M1 keys
  that named 1536 are no longer used).
- **HUD**: a frame `rts_worldgen_progress` (style `rts_hud_frame`, the minimap frame's width) under the minimap frame,
  updated every 60 ticks, removed when the fill is done or at teardown: "World: 63 % generated" ("Мир: создано 63 %").
  The first GUI run showed a longer line with chunk counts cut off at the frame's edge, a lobby with two rows called
  "Map", and "about 8 min" (39 chunks/s); all three were fixed and checked again.

### Other changes

- **Real-tile check** (after the fill; the autotest's `world_connectivity_real`): rows for at most 2,048 cells per tick
  (8 rows at 2048 as before, 4 at 4096), class counts on the way, and the flood resumable at 20,000 cells per tick. At
  4096 the old single-tick flood and class count made one 0.3-0.4 s tick (the `world` handler averaged 0.119 ms per
  tick in that minute).
- **Fog stats line**: at 4096 x 6 its per-minute counts of every force made a 75 ms tick; m3-perf's sweep (their hunk)
  spreads them over 32 ticks.

## Deviations from the contract

1. **The forced core is the spawns' discs only; city-states and ports come first in the background.** Bench at 4096 x 6
   (`world3_corebench.lua`, same plan, fresh surfaces, same minute): spawn squares radius 5 (726 chunks) 2.93-2.97 s;
   with the 8 city-state (radius 3) and 5 port (radius 1) squares (1,163 chunks) 5.49 s; the same 437 site chunks
   alone 2.50 s. A separate square costs about 0.1-0.3 s of blocking generation on top of its chunks (a contiguous
   25 x 25 block of 625 chunks: 1.92 s). The same bench at load 3: square 2.85-3.00 s, disc 2.69-2.71 s, block 1.93 s,
   so the rim of each separate region, not the load, sets the cost. In the background the spawn squares' corners and
   the site squares are done 10-16 s after the start (city-states founded 5.4-15.6 s in at 4096 behind the corners;
   1.8-9.4 s at 3072 before the corners came first; 11.6-12.5 s at 2048 in the legacy order, as in M2), before any
   army leaves home.
2. **A disc instead of the 11 x 11 square**: chunk centres within 5 chunks of the spawn's chunk, 486 instead of 726
   chunks at 4096 x 6. Bench (same plan, same minute): square 3.39-3.48 s, a disc of 5.5 chunks (582) 3.04-3.07 s, the
   disc of 5 (486) 2.73-2.83 s; an earlier bench at load 3: square 2.85-3.00 s, 5.5-disc 2.69-2.71 s. The 5.5-disc
   (the first version) took 3.1 s in a GUI client, the 5-disc 2.82 s. The chunks left out lie more than 115 tiles from
   the spawn; they come first in the background (about 6 s).
3. **Background order: site squares, then feature and label chunks, then rings.** "Ring by ring from the spawns" with
   the objectives first: every deposit, ruin, port, city-state and label of a 4096 world exists early (measured at
   4096 x 6: the last of 129 features placed 26 s into the match, at 1,401 generated chunks) instead of up to 6 minutes
   for the centre (the contested deposits lie about 40 chunk rings from the spawns), and AI planners see every site.
4. **Legacy order for 2-4 nations up to 2048** (above). Their core is M2's 13 x 13 square (676 chunks at 4 nations,
   2.8 s under load), the start median 3.5-5.0 s under load 8-20 as in M2.
5. **Default size 2048 for 2 nations** (M1: 1536), as the contract lists 2048 as the default; 1536 stays valid in configs.
6. **5-6 nations on a 2048 config** get 3072 (`world.normalize_size`) instead of being cut to 4 nations; the lobby
   prevents the combination.
7. **Labels appear with their chunk** (M2 drew all at the first refresh): no gameplay effect; at 2048 every label exists
   by about 90 s.
8. **Specialty distance at 4096** stops growing at 3072's (570 tiles); `world_specialty` accepts 450-933 path tiles for
   extended plans (M2: 300 x size/2048 to 700, which 4096 cannot meet).
9. **`world_claimable` upper bound 0.97** (was 0.95): the M2 plan of a 3072 world without sea (seed 30726) is 95.5 %
   land and marsh; a pre-existing limit of the M2 check, no plan change.
10. **Ports 5 / 6 for 5 / 6 nations** (one per nation as on a 4-nation map): with 5 ports a 6-nation 3072 world reached
    a nearest-port spread of 0.151 (limit 0.15); with 6 the four 5-6 nation worlds measured 0.029-0.113.

## For m3-ai (proposed hunks, not applied)

- `scripts/ai/towns.lua` `pick_site` and `scripts/cities.lua` `pick_site` (the latter is not this package's file):
  skip a candidate whose chunk does not exist yet and ask for it, so a 4096 AI never plans a City Center on ground that
  is generated later:
  ```lua
  local worldgen = package.loaded["__factorio-rts__/scripts/worldgen.lua"] -- or require("scripts.worldgen")
  if worldgen and not worldgen.generated(cand) then
    worldgen.request_area(C.force, {{cand.x - 64, cand.y - 64}, {cand.x + 64, cand.y + 64}})
    goto continue -- try it again on a later pass
  end
  ```
  With the background order this has not happened in any run (sites near a capital lie in the first rings; at 4096 x 6
  about 5,300 chunks, every chunk within about 14 rings = 450 tiles of a capital, exist 2 minutes in), but an AI that
  plans far may meet it.
- The economic planner (D7) reads `worldgen.progress()` and `worldgen.generated(cell)` and requests what it wants with
  `worldgen.request_area(force, area)` (at most 400 chunks a call).

## Gotchas

1. **The generation order is visible in the world.** The same plan generated in two orders differs at chunk seams
   (trees, decoratives, a few water and marsh tiles; cliffs and ores not in the bench). Byte-identical worlds need the
   same requests in the same order.
2. **A separate forced square is expensive.** Each isolated region of forced chunks costs about 0.1-0.3 s beyond its
   chunks (its partially generated rim); one contiguous block is about 3 ms per chunk under load.
3. **Single-chunk requests jump ahead of a big request.** Even after a whole-map request, a radius-0 request for a far
   chunk was done 16 s later (2048, legacy order); in the windowed order 6 s.
4. **`on_chunk_generated` of forced chunks fires inside `force_generate_chunk_requests`**: the module ignores events in
   its "core" phase and counts the core afterwards with `is_chunk_generated`.
5. **More noise slots cost nothing measurable**: the same core with 24 or 32/40 slots took 4.0-4.3 s in both trees in
   the same hour, and the terrain was identical.
6. **The slot budget decides re-rolls.** Raising it for every plan would change worlds whose first skeleton M2 had
   re-rolled; non-extended plans keep the M2 budget.

## Open issues

1. **Start time at 4096 x 6 is 7.5-7.7 s** under load: planning 2.5-3.2 s, core 2.6-2.9 s, the AIs' first layouts 2 s.
   The study's "playable 5 s after Start" needs a quieter machine or the AI layouts spread over the first ticks.
2. **Core <= 3 s holds with about 10 % margin** (2.67-2.83 s headless, 2.82 s in a GUI client): the rims of six
   separate discs cost about 0.8 s on top of the chunks (one contiguous block of 625 chunks: 1.93 s). Timing on this
   machine varies by 15 % from one minute to the next.
3. **6 AIs on 4096** were only run for the 6-7 minutes of the fill; their game (long marches, 1,300 tiles between
   capitals) is m3-ai's and m3-perf's measurement.
4. **`cities.lua` and `ai/towns.lua` may pick a site on ungenerated ground** at 4096 (proposed hunk above).
