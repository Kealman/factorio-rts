# Phase 5 M1 world (W1): implementation notes

Date: 2026-10-16. Factorio 2.0.77 (mac-arm64, Steam). Mod: `mods/factorio-rts/`. Contract: `docs/PHASE5-M1.md` §1, §3
(world.lua) and §4 (World). Study: `docs/research/scale/world-map.md`. Skirmish map generation stays in
`scripts/mapgen.lua` (`docs/qa/phase4-mapgen-notes.md`).

## Status

- **Nation mode exists.** The lobby has a **Mode** drop-down (Skirmish / Nation). Nation fixes the map type to World
  and the size by nation count: 2 nations 1536×1536, 3 or 4 nations 2048×2048. The Skirmish size and map drop-downs
  grey out. `match.normalize_config` accepts `mode = "nation"` and an optional `world_size` (1536 or 2048) that
  overrides the size by nation count; the autotests use it for 1v1 on 2048.
- **The world is generated by the engine from a Lua skeleton** (the study's hybrid). Lua plans spawns, mountain
  ranges with passes, rivers with fords, coasts, lakes, climate anchors and protected sites, and writes them as
  numeric constants into `MapGenSettings.property_expression_names`. Named noise expressions in
  `prototypes/world.lua` read them, and the engine paints the terrain with Nauvis tiles, trees, decoratives and cliffs.
- **Every plan is checked on engine samples before a chunk exists**: barrier thickness, land connectivity, start kits
  and spawn scores. A failing skeleton is re-rolled. In 60 seeds every first skeleton passed.
- **Match start is playable in at most 3.5 s at 2048** (contract: 15 s). Only a core of 13×13 chunks around each
  spawn is generated before the match starts; the rest of the map fills in the background in about 93 s of game
  time, and every ore deposit, oil field, ruin and port is placed when its chunks exist.
- **Barriers hold for every unit class.** The crossing probe orders walkers, heavies, raiders, artillery, drones and
  haulers across a range and a river mid-span: in 3 seeds all 48 cross only at a pass or a ford.
- **Results.** 60-run seed matrix 180/180; crossing probe 48/48 in 3 seeds plus 16/16 on the synced tree; AI
  autotest 1v1 normal on a 2048 world 28/28 on the synced tree (both AIs build, research, run hubs and attack; the 5
  world checks pass); restart and save/load in the middle of the fill pass; Skirmish 1v1 normal medium and the legacy
  units autotest identical line by line with and without the World package; GUI probe 4/4 twice. Zero Lua errors in
  every run.

## How a Nation match is made

`lobby.start_match` → `match.start(cfg)` with `cfg.mode == "nation"`:

1. `world.plan(c, arena_seed(m))`. Pure planning plus sampling on the never-generated surface `rts-world-sampler`.
   Returns the plan with spawns per force (rts-player first).
2. `m.arena_half = plan.half`, `m.spawns` from the plan, facing as in Skirmish.
3. `create_world`: `game.create_surface("rts-arena-<n>", world.map_gen_settings(plan))`.
4. `setup_forces`, then `world.apply(surface, plan)`: core chunks generated by force, trees, rocks and cliffs removed
   within 100 tiles of each spawn, start kits placed, chart labels drawn, the whole map requested in the background.
5. `place_bases` and `spawn_armies` exactly as in Skirmish.

### The skeleton (`world.plan`)

Per attempt, with a generator seeded from the arena seed, the attempt and the nation count:

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Coasts | 0 (30 %), 1 (45 %) or 2 (25 %) sea edges, 70–120 tiles deep with a noisy coast. A sea edge closer than 415 + depth to a spawn is dropped. |
| Spawns | On a ring of 0.32 / 0.30 / 0.31 × size for 2 / 3 / 4 nations, evenly spaced from a random angle, ±0.05 rad and ±3 % jitter. |
| Barrier rays | One per pair of angular neighbours, on their bisector, from the open centre (0.13 × size for 2 nations, 0.16 × size otherwise) out past the map edge. Ranges and rivers alternate. 3 segments each. |
| Crossings | 2 per ray (1 on a short ray) at 38 % and 78 % of its length, kept out of the sea. Pass: a disc that leaves 34–46 tiles of ground across the range. Ford: a disc of river half width + 9–12. |
| Start kit | Main patches at `match.ORE_PATCHES` in the spawn's front axes (the AI planner's layout), natural pair 70 tiles out along the front, ±13 to the sides. |
| Home deposits | A specialty 380 tiles out (72° from the outward direction), a home oil field 300 tiles out on the other side, a home lake 135 tiles outward. Positions keep clear of barriers, edges and sea. |
| Contested | On every bisector inside the open centre (0.58 / 0.62 × the centre radius): copper, iron (and coal for 3 nations; copper, iron, copper, iron for 4). An oil swamp in the middle. |
| Spurs, massifs | A short range behind each highland specialty, and up to 2 free-standing ranges in frontier regions, when they fit. Free ends: units walk around them. |
| Regions | Homes, deposits, then frontier regions by Poisson disc (300 tiles) up to 20 at 2048 (14 at 1536). Frontier climate: desert 30 %, forest 30 %, lakes 15 %, temperate 25 %. |
| Climate | Anchors shift moisture, aux and a swamp field: highland (aux up), swamp (moisture up, marsh pools), desert (moisture down), forest (moisture up), lakeland (2 small lakes). |
| Protection | Discs around spawns (175 tiles) and deposits (radius + 12): no range, river, sea or marsh inside. |

Validation on engine samples (`LuaSurface.calculate_tile_properties` on the sampler surface):

1. **Barrier thickness.** Stations every 24 tiles along every barrier (not at free ends, crossings, the edge or the
   sea), moved from warped to plain coordinates by two fixed-point steps on the sampled warp, then three 111-point
   transects (the normal and ±20°). A station's thickness is the shortest blocked run. Any segment with a station under
   `MIN_THICK` (30 tiles) is widened and measured again, up to 3 rounds. The widths grow per segment, and the pass and
   ford discs grow with them.
2. **The 8-tile grid** (65,536 cells at 2048): ford, mountain, marsh, water (elevation < −0.05) or land.
3. **Land connectivity** by a 4-connected flood from the first spawn: every spawn, every deposit (contested ones
   included) and every region centre on land.
4. **Start kits**: every main and natural patch within 150 tiles of its spawn and on land cells.
5. **Spawn scores** (study 5.1): buildable land within 150 tiles (0.35), water within 200 (0.15), defensibility from
   the land runs on the ring at 300 (0.20), expansion value (0.30): every deposit's value shared by inverse square
   path distance, divided by max(1, distance / 500), normalised by the best nation. Spread = (max − min) / max ≤ 0.15.

Then city sites, ruins, ports (from the grid), the 32-tile Voronoi region grid, region resources and chokes (crossing
centres moved to plain coordinates).

### Noise program (`prototypes/world.lua`)

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `rts_w_dx`, `rts_w_dy`, `rts_w_wx`, `rts_w_wy` | Domain warp, amplitude `rts_w_warp` = 22 tiles: basis noise at 1/260 plus 0.12 × basis noise at 1/90 |
| `rts_w_seg(px, py, ax, ay, bx, by, w)` | w minus the distance to the segment (> 0 inside the band) |
| `rts_w_mountain` | max over range segments in warped space + 3 tiles of rough edge, cut by passes and protected sites |
| `rts_w_river` | max over river segments in warped space + 1.5 tiles of rough edge |
| `rts_w_ford` | min(ford discs, river + 3): the ford covers the channel and its banks |
| `rts_w_lake`, `rts_w_coast` | lakes in warped space; the sea along edges with a noisy coast |
| `rts_w_elevation` (property `elevation`) | land ≥ 4; rivers, lakes and sea below 0 (deep water below −2); protected sites lifted above 0, planned lakes override that |
| `rts_w_moisture`, `rts_w_aux` (properties) | Nauvis moisture and aux plus the climate anchors |
| `rts_w_marsh` | swamp field with noise, pool noise, outside sites, on land |
| `rts_w_cliff_elevation`, `rts_w_cliffiness` | one cliff contour at mountain = −2.5 (cliff_elevation_0 = 20, interval 40) |

Tile probabilities: ford 400, mountain 300, marsh 250 against at most 200 for deep water and about 1 for land tiles.

|---|---|---|

### Generation, fill and placement

- **Core**: `request_to_generate_chunks(spawn, 6)` per spawn, then `force_generate_chunk_requests` (169 chunks per
  spawn, the start kit and the home lake inside).
- **Background fill**: `request_to_generate_chunks({0, 0}, size / 64)`; the engine fills about 40 chunks per second
  at 60 UPS.
- **Placement queue**: every feature (patch, oil field, ruin, port) knows its tile box. At `apply`, features whose
  chunks all exist are placed; the others wait in `storage.world.pending[chunk key]` and are placed in
  `on_chunk_generated` once their last chunk arrives. Trees, rocks, cliffs and fish in the box are removed first.
- **Ore** goes through `mapgen.place_patch(..., {land_only = true})`: exact totals, no ore on water, mountain, ford or
  marsh tiles. Each patch is recorded with `mapgen.record_patch`, so depletion alerts, the "ore remaining" report and
  `/rts-mapgen` cover the world.
- **Oil**: `crude-oil` entities on land, 9 tiles apart.
- **Ruins and ports**: `mapgen.add_ruin`, a neutral CC and two stockpiles like the Skirmish ruin, captured by the same
  rule (several ruins now). Ports carry 300 steel and 100 circuits and their own labels.
- **Fill complete**: the real-tile check runs over 32 ticks (8 grid rows per tick, `get_tile` at cell centres) and
  floods the real grid from the first spawn to every spawn, deposit, region centre and site. Whole-map tile and entity
  counts cost about 100 ms in one tick at 2048, so they run only with `world.QA_COUNTS` (the seed probe sets it).
- **Watchdog**: every 600 ticks, a fill that made no progress for 600 ticks requests the map again (a save loaded
  mid-fill).

### Crossing probe (`tools/probes/world_crossing.lua`, 1v1 on 2048, seeds 5151, 777 and 4242)

The probe stops the AI, generates the whole map and picks a mid-span lane across one range and one river: the arc
farthest from the crossings, with the nearest crossing at least 120 tiles away and clearly closer than the open inner
end. Units start 70 tiles in front of the real barrier and are ordered 70 tiles behind it. A crossing is detected in
warped coordinates, where the barrier is an exact band and every pass or ford an exact disc.

| Seed | Range lane: real width, nearest pass | River lane: real width, nearest ford | Units | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5151 | 53 tiles, 233 along | 37 tiles, 200 along | 2 drones, walker, heavy, raider, artillery, hauler per lane | 16/16, every crossing at a pass or ford |
| 777 | 50, 175 | 46, 165 | same | 16/16 |
| 4242 | 41, 160 | 39, 164 | same | 16/16 |

Arrival times (seed 777): range: hauler 21 s, raider 29 s, drones 36 s, walker 37 s, artillery 51 s, heavy 63 s;
river: hauler 18 s, raider 25 s, drones 26 s, walker 32 s, artillery 48 s, heavy 56 s.

**The first version failed** (seed 5151, before the fix): the artillery crossed the river and the range mid-span.
What was learned:

- **Domain warp thinned the bands.** A second warp octave of 0.35 at 1/70 squeezed planned 28–34-tile barriers to
  about 14–18 tiles on diagonals (ASCII dumps of the real tiles at the crossing points).
- **Clean bands hold at every width.** Closed boxes with one band of rts-mountain or deep water across the whole
  box: with no gap, no unit moves at any width from 16 to 64 (no path). With a 40-tile gap 100 tiles away, and with
  one 1,000 tiles away, every class went through the gap at every width from 16 to 56. So the pathfinder respects
  both tiles, and the artillery's long legs only matter where a band is thin.
- **Spider units walk straight while their long path is pending.** In a world chunk set that was only partly
  generated, the heavy and the artillery walked straight to the river and stood with their bodies over deep water.
- **The fix**: a gentler warp (second octave 0.12 at 1/90), wider bands (ranges 36–42, rivers 34–40 tiles nominal) and
  the sampled thickness check that widens every segment to at least 30 real tiles. Phase 4 measured water of 14
  tiles stopping every unit; 30 leaves a 2× margin.

## Gotchas

1. **Domain warp changes widths, not just shapes.** A steep warp squeezes a band. Barriers are therefore measured on
   samples in plain coordinates and widened where thin.
2. **Spider units walk straight while a long path is computed.** A barrier must be thick enough that their legs
   cannot bridge it even then.
3. **`calculate_tile_properties` works on a surface that never generates a chunk**, and its `map_gen_settings` can be
   rewritten freely. The planner uses one sampler surface, `rts-world-sampler`, for every attempt and match.
4. **Chart-mode text with `scale_with_zoom = true` floods the HUD minimap**: the names keep their screen size at the
   minimap's zoom. The labels now have a fixed world size (region names about 16 tiles tall).
5. **A headless server never charts** (study gotcha 2), so fog is verified in the GUI probe.
6. **`pgrep -f` in a wait loop matches the loop's own command line** when the pattern appears in it.
7. **`LuaProfiler` values cannot be compared in Lua.** The 15-second check reads the logged durations
   (`tools/world-seeds.sh`).
8. **Highland-dark-rock carries `sprite_usage_surface = "gleba"`**; the copy clears it so the sprites load on the
   world surface.
9. **Whole-map counts are expensive**: `count_tiles_filtered` and `count_entities_filtered` over 2048² took 98 ms in
   one tick. They are QA-only.
10. **Chunk generation requests survive a save.** A save taken mid-fill finishes its fill after the load without
    help; the watchdog stays as a safety net.

## Open issues

- **Mountain coverage is light**: 1.6 % of a 2048 map against 5.9 % in the study probe. W1 has ray barriers, spurs and
  massifs; W2 can add secondary ranges and rivers inside sectors.
- **Region patch depletion alerts go to every force** (`mapgen` sends forceless patches to every participant).
- **The HUD minimap shows the whole world** at about 0.1 pixels per tile; the regional minimap is W2.
- **Nearest-enemy path spread** reaches 0.22 on some 3-nation seeds (informational; the contract checks the score
  spread).
- **Uranium and rare deposits** are not placed (W2, behind Geology research).
- **`world.MIN_THICK` (30)** is measured against today's units. A new, larger spider unit needs the crossing probe
  again.
- **Nation fog** charts only around the capital; the study's "Survey" start option is W2.
