Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Guided objectives: a strip of seven first steps for your first Skirmish and first Nation match,
      with a "Guided objectives" switch in the lobby.
    - Escape opens the game menu (save, load, settings, exit) and closes an open RTS panel first.
    - The selected haulers' routes are drawn on the map while the hauler panel is open.
  Changes:
    - Nation AI reaches Age III sooner: a concrete chain, district order, engine focus and science hold
      (Age III by minute 40 in 2 of 5 test seeds, was 1 of 5; the target of 3 is not met yet). (#1)
    - Nation AI at war: siege railways for artillery trains, interceptor patrols, bomber strikes and war
      research first. In 60-minute matches it still fields no interceptors, bombers or artillery trains. (#4)
    - Nation AI in debt retires idle units and stops recruiting while it loses money. (#5)
    - Hard Nation AI plans more housing and goes for a second city-state; no resource bonus. (#3)
    - Nation AI rail programs: kits from the works factory, crews in turn, coal for waiting crews, rebuilt
      depots after a war, and rail crafts leave 100 stone in the capital. The aggressive AI opens its first
      line sooner; linking three cities is still slower than designed. (#2, #7)
    - An AI freight train carries everything its town offers. (#7)
    - A hauler pays its way back before it leaves, so it no longer strands at a drop-off without coal;
      auto haulers wait up to a minute for a load worth the trip's coal. (#6)
    - Rail crews keep clear of mountain rims and go home or are recalled when stuck. (#2)
    - RTS buildings that looked alike on Factorio 2.1's new art get distinct tints. (#11)
  Balancing:
    - Air Force 30 industrial kits (was 50), Military railways 17 (was 25).
  Optimizations:
    - Nation periodic handlers run on their own phases, the market AI's ledger read runs on the scheduler
      and AI target scans are cached: far fewer script ticks over 3 ms. (#8)
  Bugfixes:
    - A right click on a pentapod's leg attacks the pentapod. (#12)
    - Escape no longer leaves the remote view for a tick. (#12)
    - The stockpile panel colours a count against this hub's own Keep. (#10)
  Gui:
    - The engine's hauler lines on the map are drawn at their 1 px minimum without destination markers. (#9)
    - The Nation lobby's world-generation estimate uses Factorio 2.1's faster rate.
  Locale:
    - Russian review: one glossary, the game's own item names and shorter panel lines. (#17)
  Info:
    - Linux: the headless autotests pass on x86-64 and ARM64. Windows is untested; reports are welcome. (#14, #15)
    - Issues: https://github.com/Kealman/factorio-rts/issues

Version: 0.1.0
  Major Features:
    - First public release.
    - Skirmish: an RTS on Factorio. A free camera, box selection, right-click orders, control groups,
      abilities and a Commander hero, with an army built through a real Factorio production economy.
    - Skirmish: 1 to 3 AI commanders with economic, aggressive or turtle doctrines on generated arenas.
    - Nation: a mini-state on a world of 2048, 3072 or 4096 tiles with 2 to 6 nations, regions,
      city-states, trade ports and ruins.
    - Nation: cities and territory, people and labor, rails and trains, a treasury, a world market
      and diplomacy with wars, treaties and victory modes.
    - Nation: three ages, from the Railway Charter to the Space Program and a Space victory.
    - Nation: divisions with supply, city capture, garrisons, fronts, fort belts, air units,
      anti-aircraft turrets, and artillery and armoured trains.
    - Nation: a layered nation AI whose difficulty changes skill, never resources.
  Features:
    - Divisions and demolishing your own buildings work in both modes.
    - English and Russian texts.
  Compatibility:
    - Requires Factorio 2.1. Space Age is optional: with it units use the pentapod graphics,
      rail lines can cross water on bridges and food spoils.
    - Designed to run alone in a new Freeplay game. Other mods are untested.
  Info:
    - Known issues and the full feature list: https://github.com/Kealman/factorio-rts
