Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-10-26
  Features:
    - Factory and barracks recipe buttons order crafts: click = 1, Shift = 5, Ctrl = as many as the network can pay,
      right click takes crafts off the order. The button and the status line show the crafts left and a finished
      order stops the machine. A new Endless toggle in the recipe grid keeps the old endless production. (#22)
    - Belts, underground belts, splitters, inserters and medium poles can be selected (click, or a box with no
      units in it) and demolished with Delete or the Demolish button: two presses, half the recipe back. (#23)
    - Placed ghosts can be selected (click or box) and cancelled at once with Delete, Backspace or Cancel. (#27)
    - The factory crafts underground belts and splitters, and in Nation mode long-handed inserters; the economy
      build menu has them. (#25)
    - Units clear trees and rocks: right click a tree or rock, or Shift + right-drag over an area. Rocks pay their
      stone and coal to the nearest stockpile. A ghost placed over trees marks them for the construction robots. (#24)
    - A new city's hub and city panels say "N ghosts waiting: nothing to build them with" and raise an alert when
      its robots have nothing to build with. (#26)
    - Help cards: hold Shift over a building, unit or ghost (or press Shift+F11) for what it is, how it works, what
      it takes and makes, its ranges, needs and a tip. A "?" button on building, hub, depot and city panels opens
      the same card, and build menu tooltips carry a one-line description. (#30)
    - The refinery, chemical plant and pumpjack panels say that oil travels through the network as items: no pipes,
      no rotation. A pumpjack outside every network shows "no network" and raises an alert. (#28)
    - Trains run stop lists: right click depots to add stops, each with its own orders (unload to Keep, everything
      or chosen items; load exports or chosen items; troops board or get off; bombard here) and the engine's wait
      conditions. The list loops by itself. (#31)
    - The train builder composes trains carriage by carriage from presets or up to 5 saved templates: mixed cargo,
      troop and artillery trains, armoured ones, longer trains on 60- or 80-tile platforms. A standing train
      couples or drops a carriage at its depot. (#32)
    - The Rail tool (Economy tab) lays your own track: click waypoints, with curves, branches, a bill per segment
      and undo. Robots and construction trains build it, and chain signals keep trains on single track apart. (#31)
    - Rail stations: small depots without a platform that put their stops on your track. Rail signals and chain
      signals are in the build menu. (#31)
    - Auto lines run along your own track where it helps, and a depot takes any number of lines. (#31)
  Changes:
    - A new city's settlers bring raw goods, a factory, two houses and a farm from the mother city's network, as far
      as it holds them; nothing is created. AI nations are unchanged. (#26)
    - Your supply haulers wait for a fuller load of rations, goods and magazines while the town holds at least half
      its Keep, 4 minutes at most. Below half they leave at once. AI unchanged. (#21)
    - Trains of 0.1.1 saves load as two-stop lists (their pickup and drop-off), and the Station and the signals are
      unlocked for nations that already had Railways.
  Balancing:
    - New recipes: underground belt (10 iron + 5 belts, makes 2), splitter (10 iron + 5 copper + 4 belts),
      long-handed inserter (15 iron + 5 copper, makes 5), rail station (20 iron, 4 steel, 4 copper), rail signal and
      chain signal (5 iron + 1 circuit each).
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed "Unknown key: recipe-name.rts-oil-processing" and nine more recipe names in the refinery, chemical plant
      and factory panels: every recipe has an explicit English and Russian name. (#29)
    - The objectives of the other mode no longer log "step done" in a match (log only). (#19)
  Gui:
    - The help card from a Shift hover appears about 0.3 s after the press and closes with a click, after 20 s or
      on the next Shift press; the engine does not report the key release.
    - R on a refinery or chemical plant says that oil travels through the network. (#28)
  Info:
    - Rail limits: two trains that each want the platform the other holds wait for each other (the train panel says
      "single track needs a passing loop or a second track"), and junctions turn one way, so a branch entered from
      the other side needs a second segment. (#31)
    - The pumpjack still shows the vanilla art's baked pipe stub; the panels and tooltips say it has no pipes.
    - The AI pace items (#2, #20) and Windows (#15) move to 0.1.3.

Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Guided objectives: a strip of seven first steps for your first Skirmish and first Nation match,
      with a "Guided objectives" switch in the lobby.
    - Escape opens the game menu (save, load, settings, exit) and closes an open RTS panel first.
    - The selected haulers' routes are drawn on the map while the hauler panel is open.
  Changes:
    - Nation AI reaches Age III sooner: a concrete chain, district order, engine focus and science hold
      (Age III by minute 40 in 2 of 5 test seeds, was 1 of 5; the target of 3 is not met yet). (#1)
    - Nation AI at war: siege railways for artillery trains, interceptor patrols, bomber strikes and war
      research first. In 60-minute matches it still fields no interceptors, bombers or artillery trains. (#4)
    - Nation AI in debt retires idle units and stops recruiting while it loses money. (#5)
    - Hard Nation AI plans more housing and goes for a second city-state; no resource bonus. (#3)
    - Nation AI rail programs: kits from the works factory, crews in turn, coal for waiting crews, rebuilt
      depots after a war, and rail crafts leave 100 stone in the capital. The aggressive AI opens its first
      line sooner; linking three cities is still slower than designed. (#2, #7)
    - An AI freight train carries everything its town offers. (#7)
    - A hauler pays its way back before it leaves, so it no longer strands at a drop-off without coal;
      auto haulers wait up to a minute for a load worth the trip's coal. (#6)
    - Rail crews keep clear of mountain rims and go home or are recalled when stuck. (#2)
    - RTS buildings that looked alike on Factorio 2.1's new art get distinct tints. (#11)
  Balancing:
    - Air Force 30 industrial kits (was 50), Military railways 17 (was 25).
  Optimizations:
    - Nation periodic handlers run on their own phases, the market AI's ledger read runs on the scheduler
      and AI target scans are cached: far fewer script ticks over 3 ms. (#8)
  Bugfixes:
    - A right click on a pentapod's leg attacks the pentapod. (#12)
    - Escape no longer leaves the remote view for a tick. (#12)
    - The stockpile panel colours a count against this hub's own Keep. (#10)
  Gui:
    - The engine's hauler lines on the map are drawn at their 1 px minimum without destination markers. (#9)
    - The Nation lobby's world-generation estimate uses Factorio 2.1's faster rate.
  Locale:
    - Russian review: one glossary, the game's own item names and shorter panel lines. (#17)
  Info:
    - Linux: the headless autotests pass on x86-64 and ARM64. Windows is untested; reports are welcome. (#14, #15)
    - Issues: https://github.com/Kealman/factorio-rts/issues

Version: 0.1.0
  Major Features:
    - First public release.
    - Skirmish: an RTS on Factorio. A free camera, box selection, right-click orders, control groups,
      abilities and a Commander hero, with an army built through a real Factorio production economy.
    - Skirmish: 1 to 3 AI commanders with economic, aggressive or turtle doctrines on generated arenas.
    - Nation: a mini-state on a world of 2048, 3072 or 4096 tiles with 2 to 6 nations, regions,
      city-states, trade ports and ruins.
    - Nation: cities and territory, people and labor, rails and trains, a treasury, a world market
      and diplomacy with wars, treaties and victory modes.
    - Nation: three ages, from the Railway Charter to the Space Program and a Space victory.
    - Nation: divisions with supply, city capture, garrisons, fronts, fort belts, air units,
      anti-aircraft turrets, and artillery and armoured trains.
    - Nation: a layered nation AI whose difficulty changes skill, never resources.
  Features:
    - Divisions and demolishing your own buildings work in both modes.
    - English and Russian texts.
  Compatibility:
    - Requires Factorio 2.1. Space Age is optional: with it units use the pentapod graphics,
      rail lines can cross water on bridges and food spoils.
    - Designed to run alone in a new Freeplay game. Other mods are untested.
  Info:
    - Known issues and the full feature list: https://github.com/Kealman/factorio-rts
